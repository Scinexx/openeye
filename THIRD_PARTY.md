# Third-party material

- **Item sprite sheet, item list and class sprites** (`public/catalog/items.png`, class PNGs) come from the [Open-RotMG-Website](https://github.com/OpenRotMG/Open-RotMG-Website) repository (GPL-3.0). Item and class definitions in `public/catalog/*.json`, including the item tooltips, are derived from `data/objects.xml` in [Open-RotMG-Server](https://github.com/OpenRotMG/Open-RotMG-Server) (AGPL-3.0). The original game art belongs to its creators.
- **Potion and key sounds** (`public/sfx/use_potion.mp3`, `public/sfx/use_key.mp3`) are the game's own `use_potion` and `use_key` sound effects from `data/sfx` in [Open-RotMG-Server](https://github.com/OpenRotMG/Open-RotMG-Server) (AGPL-3.0).
- **Pixelify Sans**, **Atkinson Hyperlegible** and **Source Sans 3** fonts are under the SIL Open Font License; license texts are in `public/fonts/`.
- Data shown in OpenEye comes from the public Open RotMG game API.

OpenEye is a fan project and is not affiliated with Open RotMG, DECA or Kabam.
